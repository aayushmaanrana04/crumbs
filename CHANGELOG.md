# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First public release of Crumbs (previously developed as Summaryse, then Carrel).

- **Save pages** from the toolbar side panel, ⌘⇧S / Ctrl+Shift+S, or the right-click menu. Selected text is saved as a highlight, and duplicates are recognised, including pages that declare a canonical address. The save confirmation has a View button that opens the side panel.
- **Summaries** with the AI provider you choose: a gist and key points, each cited to the sentence on the page; steps, tables and code copied exactly; a note when charts or code are only images; and suggested tags. Long pages are summarised in parts and merged.
- **Side panel:** the page's full summary once saved, related pages from your library with the words they share, quick save, notes, and Ask about the page. "Follow my browsing" is offered when you switch tabs, and can be dismissed.
- **Library:** filters, keyword search and search by meaning, reading view with highlights, tags, notes and projects.
- **Ask** across your library or a project, with sources.
- **Providers:** sign in with OpenRouter, or use your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a local Ollama. Recommended models are preselected (Claude Haiku 5.5 for summaries), with an override for any other model.
- **Privacy:** everything is stored in the browser, there's no Crumbs server or analytics, and you can export or delete everything. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
